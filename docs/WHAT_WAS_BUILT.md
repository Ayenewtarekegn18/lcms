## 🎯 Usage Quick Start

### To Register a Member:
1. Click "+ Add Member" on Dashboard
2. Fill in Step 1: Personal information
3. Click "Next" → Go to Step 2
4. Fill in Step 2: Academic status
5. Click "Next" → Go to Step 3
6. Fill in Step 3: Marital status
7. Click "Next" → Go to Step 4
8. Fill in Step 4: Church history
9. Click "Next" → Go to Step 5
10. Fill in Step 5: Church engagement
11. Click "Submit Registration"
12. Member appears in Members list

### To Manage Members:
1. Go to Members page
2. Search for member
3. Click 👁️ to view details
4. Click ✏️ to edit
5. Click 🗑️ to delete

### To Add Announcements:
1. Go to Communications
2. Click "New Announcement"
3. Fill in title and content
4. Select recipients
5. Choose channels
6. Click "Publish Announcement"
